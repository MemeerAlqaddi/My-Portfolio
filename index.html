About me

I work where design, systems, and business meet.

My work sits at the intersection of UX architecture, digital product development, Salesforce, cloud systems, and business strategy. I am most useful when a problem cannot be solved by one discipline alone.

I like understanding the whole system - what the user is trying to do, what the business needs to happen, what the technology has to support, and where friction appears between those layers. From there, I can move from concept and architecture into interface, implementation, integration, testing, and refinement.
